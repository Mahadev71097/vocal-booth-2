# Vocal Booth

Remove the music from a song and keep the singer's voice, right in your browser. Then make the voice louder and clearer, add echo and bass, and save it as MP3, FLAC or WAV.

Open it: https://mahadev71097.github.io/vocal-booth-2/

Everything runs on your own device. Songs are never uploaded anywhere.

## Credits

- Voice separation: [Spleeter](https://github.com/deezer/spleeter) 2-stem model by Deezer (MIT licence), in the ONNX form published by [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx).
- Runs on [ONNX Runtime Web](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime) (MIT licence).
- MP3 files are made with [LAME](https://lame.sourceforge.io) (LGPL) through [wasm-media-encoders](https://github.com/arseneyr/wasm-media-encoders) (MIT licence).
